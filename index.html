What you get

Every meeting ends in one page.

Lapel is not a recorder. Its AI understands the meeting: who committed to what, what changed, and what is still open. Then it writes the page and files it on your calendar.

Decisions

Every decision in one line, in the words the room agreed on. No transcript to dig through.

Owners and deadlines

Who owes what, and by when. Each commitment is tied to the person who made it.

Follow-ups, drafted

A message to the room, written and waiting. Read it, change a word, send it on WhatsApp or email.

Filed on your calendar

Every meeting, with every note and detail, is added to its calendar event. Open Tuesday at 11 and the whole meeting is there.

Open questions

What was raised and left undecided, so it comes back to the table next time.

Ask it later

Search any past meeting in plain language. What did we agree with the Chennai distributor?

Why it has a camera

A recorder only hears. Lapel also sees.

Half of what gets decided in a room is never said aloud. It is drawn, shown or pointed at. The camera lets the AI read it, and Lapel stores it with the meeting.

Questions

What leaders ask first.

Straight answers on recording, privacy and the pilot.

Does everyone know they are being recorded?

Yes. A light on the pin is on whenever Lapel is recording, and we ask you to tell the room. Lapel is not built for secret recording.

What if my company does not allow recording?

We give you a short policy note for your legal and IT teams. You choose which meetings Lapel joins.

Does it recognise faces?

No. Lapel knows who was in a meeting from your calendar and from what you tell it.

Which calendars does it work with?

The pilot starts with Google Calendar and Outlook. Tell us what you use when you apply.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Hindi and Hinglish.

Does it work for online meetings?

Lapel is built for in-person meetings, the ones other tools miss.

How does it stay on my jacket?

A magnetic clasp holds it through the fabric. There is no needle, so it leaves no mark.

Is the pilot pin the final product?

No. The pilot uses an early pin. The final pin is being designed, and pilot members get first claim on it.

What happens to my data if I leave the pilot?

We delete it when you ask, and confirm in writing.

Anyone who appears in a Lapel recording can write to rohit@lapel.pro to ask us to delete the parts that relate to them.
